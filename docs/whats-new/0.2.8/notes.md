Signatures that look like yours: colour any word, link any line, and keep as many as you need.
- Always show quoted text: open the earlier messages a reply or forward carries, instead of folding them (Settings → Appearance).
- Always show signatures, the same way.
## Colour any word in your signature
media: signature.webp
Select words in your signature and make them bold, italic, a link or any colour. Your name can be green, your motto gold and your website a link, all on one line.
- Several signatures per account: pick one for new messages and one for replies and forwards.
- Switch signatures in any message, or add one back after leaving it off.
- Your current signature stays as it is.
