Questions

Before you take off.

Which email accounts work?
Gmail and Google Workspace, Outlook and Microsoft 365, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail, Zoho, GMX, AOL and any IMAP server. Type your address and Kite works out the rest, with an app password walkthrough where one is needed. Add as many accounts as you like and see them in one list.
Where does my mail live?
In a local database on your computer, next to its attachments. Your provider still keeps the original; Kite keeps a copy so lists and search answer instantly, and Disconnect removes everything tied to that account.
Do I need AI to use it?
No. Everything works without it. Connect your own key (Claude, OpenAI or a local model) and Summarise, Draft and Rewrite appear as buttons. They only see the emails you picked, every call is listed in Settings → Privacy, and you can set a daily and monthly spend limit.
"Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?
That's SmartScreen, shown for apps without a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.
What about Mac?
The desktop app is Windows for now. Tell me if you'd use it on a Mac.
